Tile Trove privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Applies to Tile Trove - Triple Match for Android (com.yongaworks.tiletrove) · Developer: Yonga Works · Contact: support@yongaworks.com

Tile Trove is a tile-matching puzzle game. There is no account to create and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. This page explains what the game keeps on your phone, what the services built into it receive, and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress: level, coins, boosters, album items, daily puzzle streak and settings. It is stored in the game's private storage on your device.

Services built into the game

The game uses the following Google services. Each one receives the data described below and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob shows ads. It may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics, for showing ads, measuring them and preventing fraud. Whether ads are personalised depends on the choice you make in the consent message (see below).
  • Google User Messaging Platform shows the consent message where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states) and stores your choice on the device.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase counts how the game is played, for example which levels are started, won or lost, which boosters are used and whether an ad or purchase was completed. It uses an app-instance identifier and device information, and the advertising ID when it is available. We use these numbers only to improve the game's difficulty, balance and stability.
  • Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust game settings (such as rewards and difficulty help) without an update. It uses a Firebase installation identifier.
  • Google Play Billing processes in-app purchases. Payment details are handled by Google; we receive only the information needed to deliver what you bought (product, order identifier and purchase token).
  • Google Play Games Services backs up your progress to your Google account (saved games) when you are signed in to Play Games on your device, so it can be restored on a new phone. It uses your Play Games player identifier.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: where a consent message is shown, you can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (the shield button).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Google › Ads).
  • Ads: the "No Ads" purchase removes interstitial ads.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the game deletes the progress stored on your phone. Saved games in Play Games can be deleted from the Play Games app or your Google account. Data received by the services above is kept as described under Retention below.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run our own servers for the game; we do not receive or store your personal information ourselves.
  • The game does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or file permissions.

Retention

Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics settings and for no longer than 14 months. Other data is kept by the services above according to their own policies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Data sent by the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
